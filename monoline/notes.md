# Monoline

Flowers from the Garden's climates put in glass, drawn with one pen on three angles: ikebana drawn as a metro
map. A card on delineate's page that grows an arrangement, takes it back into its bottle, turns the bottle into
the next, and grows the next.

- `pen.ts`: every mark (`line`, `run`, `dot`, `block`, `leaf`, `path`).
- `vessel.ts`: the bottles, and glass drawn layer by layer as light passes.
- `plants.ts`: the plants by garden, each with its role, its real colours and its drawing.
- `arrange.ts`: a garden, a bottle and its stems from one seed. `palette.ts`: the colours for them.
- `grow.ts`, `show.ts`: the motion, and the card that keeps itself.
- `npm run monoline`: the card at http://localhost:5350/, with `?seed=`, `?sheet` and `?layers`.

## Rules

1. **Colour by structure.** Flowers inside the colours their plants grow in; the card across the wheel from
   the hero, split from there, or the hero's hue taken deep; clear of the stems' green and the accent; an
   accent 60 degrees or more from the hero. Lightness in OKLCH, one ladder for every roll.
2. **Stems never cross.** They leave the mouth in the order their heads stand, spread across it, on the
   pen's three angles.
3. **What rides a form comes from the form.** Highlights and shaded lines follow the walls.
4. **Bottles share their points**, eight a side, so any one becomes any other.
5. **Out of view, nothing moves**; in view, motion keeps the screen's rate.
