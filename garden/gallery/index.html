The garden · tokens

Every color and plant this garden is drawn with, by day and by night, read straight from the files that ship: src/tokens.css, src/garden/paint.css, src/garden/plants.json, and the plant drawings.

Palette

Paper and ink, and every token this garden's drawings reach. Each color sits on its own paper, day and night.

Pen

One pen at every plant size. The lit edge of a shape takes the thin line and the shadow edge the thick one.

Plants

In order of rank, the order in which a crowded screen keeps them.
